This product offers downside protection while preserving your upside potential.

📈 市場環境說明
Given the current macroeconomic environment, a more defensive positioning may be prudent.
鑑於目前的總體經濟環境，較為防禦性的配置可能較為穩健。
市場波動時向客戶說明策略調整，避免直接做買賣建議。

I specialize in convertible bond strategies and have over 25 years of cross-disciplinary experience across securities, insurance, and banking.

💡 客觀表達看法（不做推薦）
Based on the data we've reviewed, my assessment is that the risk-reward ratio is not particularly favorable at current levels.
根據我們審閱的數據，我的評估是目前水位的風險報酬比不算特別理想。
避免直接推薦買賣，用「我的評估」和「風險報酬比」客觀表達看法。

🎙 面試英文問答實戰
❓ Tell me about yourself.
最重要的開場，建議完整背誦
My name is Rex. I have over 25 years of experience in financial services, spanning securities, insurance, and banking — a background that gives me a truly comprehensive view of the market. I hold a CFP certification and specialize in convertible bond strategies and data-driven wealth management. I also run an investment education platform with a substantial following in Taiwan. I'm joining your bank because I believe my cross-disciplinary expertise and client-first philosophy align perfectly with your approach to wealth management.
❓ Why do you want this role?
展示動機真誠、與公司契合
I'm drawn to this role for two main reasons. First, working within a global institution gives me access to world-class products, research, and best practices — which ultimately benefits the clients I serve. Second, I've spent my career building deep expertise across multiple disciplines. This role lets me apply that integrated knowledge in a high-caliber environment where client relationships are truly valued.
❓ What is your greatest strength?
強調你最獨特的跨領域競爭優勢
My greatest strength is my cross-disciplinary perspective. Unlike advisors who specialize in a single area, I have worked across securities, insurance, and banking — allowing me to provide truly holistic financial advice. Combined with my deep expertise in convertible bond strategies and a data-driven analytical approach, I bring a rare combination of breadth, depth, and real-world experience.
❓ How do you handle difficult clients?
展示同理心與解決問題的能力
I've found that most "difficult" client situations stem from unmet expectations or a lack of transparency. My approach is always to listen first, without becoming defensive. I make sure the client feels heard, then I address their concerns with facts and data. If a client is unhappy about short-term performance, I'll review the original rationale, explain the broader market context, and if necessary, revisit whether their portfolio still aligns with their stated goals.
❓ Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
展示清晰目標與長期承諾
In five years, I see myself having built a strong, loyal client base within the bank — clients who trust me not just as an advisor, but as a long-term financial partner. I also hope to contribute to the growth of the wealth management team, potentially mentoring junior advisors and sharing the cross-disciplinary perspective I've developed over my career.
